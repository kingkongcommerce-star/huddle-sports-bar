The Spot

This is where the crew watches the game

The Huddle Sports Bar is a modern sports bar in the heart of District 1, Ho Chi Minh City, open daily from 2:00 PM to 3:00 AM. We serve delicious food, ice-cold beer, handcrafted cocktails, premium spirits, and coffee in a fun, welcoming spot for locals, expats and travelers to hang around, catch a match, and stay for a few rounds.

As a sports bar for expats, travelers and sports fans in Saigon, every big screen is tuned to the game — English Premier League, Formula 1, boxing, UFC — with pool and darts running all night for the group that wants more than just a seat at the bar.

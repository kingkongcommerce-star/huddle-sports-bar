The Spot

This is where the crew watches the game

The Huddle Sports Bar is a modern sports bar in the heart of District 1, Saigon. Open from day to night, we serve delicious food, ice-cold beer, handcrafted cocktails, premium spirits, and coffee in a fun, welcoming spot built for friends to hang around, catch a match, and stay for a few rounds.

Every big screen is tuned to the game — English Premier League, La Liga, Formula 1, boxing, UFC — with pool and darts running all night for the group that wants more than just a seat at the bar.
